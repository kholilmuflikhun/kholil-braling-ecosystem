# Application Ownership

| Application | Primary responsibility | Domain ownership |
|---|---|---|
| Growth OS | Business oversight and reporting | CRM views, finance operations, KPI, marketing, projects, analytics |
| Production POS | Printing operations | POS, work orders, production stages, inventory operations, purchasing |
| Personal Website | Public acquisition and publishing | Public profile, portfolio, services, articles, inquiry capture |
| Braling E-Commerce | Online sales channel | Storefront, cart, checkout, customer self-service |

Shared domains such as identity, catalog, pricing, customer identity, sales orders, and payment records must have one authoritative service and explicit write ownership. Applications must not duplicate authoritative business records.
